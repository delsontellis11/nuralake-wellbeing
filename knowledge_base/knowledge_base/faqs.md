# Well Beings Spa — Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking
- How do I book? Reply to this message with your preferred date, time, and treatment. We'll confirm within 1 hour.
- Can I walk in? Walk-ins are welcome but subject to availability. Booking in advance is recommended.
- How far in advance should I book? We recommend 24–48 hours in advance, especially on weekends.

## Cancellation Policy
- Cancellations made 24+ hours before appointment: full refund
- Cancellations within 24 hours: 50% charge
- No-shows: full charge applies

## Payment
- We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and Apple Pay
- 50% deposit required for group bookings and packages

## Preparing for Your Visit
- Arrive 10–15 minutes early to complete a consultation form
- Avoid heavy meals 1–2 hours before treatment
- We provide robes, slippers, and towels
- Please inform us of any medical conditions or allergies

## Gift Cards
- Gift cards available for any amount from AED 100
- Valid for 6 months from purchase
- Can be purchased in-spa or via WhatsApp

## Hygiene & Safety
- All equipment is sterilised between sessions
- Fresh linen used for every client
- Therapists are certified and trained professionals
