# Well Beings Spa - Location and Hours

## Location
- Address: Shop 4, Marina Promenade Tower, Dubai Marina, Dubai, UAE
- Landmark: Next to Dubai Marina Mall
- Parking: Valet at Marina Promenade. Public parking at P1 Dubai Marina Mall (AED 5/hour)
- Metro: Dubai Marina Metro Station (Red Line) - 5 minute walk

## Opening Hours
- Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- Public Holidays: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM

## Getting Here
- From Downtown Dubai: 15-20 minutes via Sheikh Zayed Road
- From Dubai Airport: 30-35 minutes by car or Metro
- From Palm Jumeirah: 10 minutes by car
