# Well Beings Spa - FAQs

## Booking
- How do I book? Reply with your preferred date, time, and treatment. We confirm within 1 hour.
- Can I walk in? Walk-ins welcome but booking recommended.
- How far in advance? 24-48 hours recommended, especially weekends.

## Cancellation Policy
- 24+ hours before: full refund
- Within 24 hours: 50% charge
- No-shows: full charge

## Payment
- We accept cash, credit/debit cards, and Apple Pay
- 50% deposit required for group bookings

## Preparing for Your Visit
- Arrive 10-15 minutes early
- Avoid heavy meals 1-2 hours before treatment
- We provide robes, slippers, and towels
- Inform us of any medical conditions or allergies

## Gift Cards
- Available from AED 100, valid 6 months
